### Abstract summary

A reference architecture for cislunar data and autonomy services that co-designs communications, storage, and edge compute for long light-time, intermittent visibility, asymmetric bandwidth, and planned outages. The architecture combines delay/disruption-tolerant networking, contact-plan scheduling, hierarchical caching, replicated state, compute-near-data execution, provenance metadata, job receipts, policy descriptors, and multi-actor interoperability across surface nodes, relays, and Earth gateways.

The paper evaluates downlink-first, edge-first, and hybrid operating paradigms using mission-relevant metrics including latency to actionable insight, deadline success, storage-driven data-loss risk, and compute energy per delivered informational product.
