# Photography notes

I shoot mostly for fun, sometimes for work.

## Gear

- **Sony A7 IV** — main body, 35/1.8 + 50/1.4 + 90 macro
- **DJI Mavic 3** — aerial, landscapes
- **iPhone 15 Pro** — everything in between

## Edit flow

Raw → Lightroom (exposure, WB, lens corrections) → occasional Photoshop for cleanup → export 3000px long edge JPEGs for web.

## Style

Subdued, cinematic palettes. Blue hour and golden hour are both fair game. I'd rather have one frame I love than ten I tolerate.
